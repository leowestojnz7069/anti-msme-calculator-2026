## What the Calculator Does

Anti-Cheat Calculator is a browser-accessible finance tool for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises. Through its Chinese-language interface, users can calculate operating figures and organize information used to review business performance and planning assumptions.

Its calculations follow concepts associated with Chinese Accounting Standards, while its business-plan review takes an academic approach to feasibility assessment. The application can serve business owners, planners, students, and others who work with MSME operating data.
